Challenge #0 – BestBurger Triple-Stop

Objective: Visit any three different BestBurger restaurants, enjoy your favourite burger combo at each stop, and snap a proof photo!

How to Complete the Challenge

  1. Select 3 restaurants from the “BestBurger Locations” list below.
  2. Order any meal (classic, deluxe, or plant-based — your pick!).
  3. Take a clear photo that shows:
    • you (or your group) inside/outside the BestBurger branch, and
    • your meal or the BestBurger logo in frame.
  4. Keep the receipt; the order number is required at submission.
  5. After visiting all three spots, upload your photos & receipts via the submission form.
  6. Our validators will review within 48 h. Once approved, claim your reward!

BestBurger Locations

  • Downtown Flagship101 Main St, Central City
  • Riverside Drive-Thru455 River Pkwy, North Bank
  • University District33 Campus Ave, College Town
  • Seaside Boardwalk890 Ocean Blvd, Bayview
  • Historic Market Square12 Market Ln, Old Quarter

Photo Tips

  • Shoot in daylight near a window for the best burger glow.
  • Avoid motion blur — prop your phone or use a timer.
  • Include the BestBurger neon sign or counter for easy verification.
  • Landscape orientation shows more of the venue, but portrait works too.
